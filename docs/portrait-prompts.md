# Resident portrait prompts

These four fictional-person portraits were generated with the built-in image generation tool under the `photorealistic-natural` use case. Each source PNG was resized to a 900-pixel-long-side JPEG for the browser UI.

## Mara Voss

> Create one candid, believable photograph of a fictional human resident, Mara Voss, a woman mechanic in her early 40s in an Earth-like near-future city. Chest-up, looking just slightly off camera as if speaking to a stranded explorer on the sidewalk. Weathered olive work jacket, practical dark hair tied back, a few grease marks on the sleeve, natural face and skin texture, subtle asymmetry and lived-in expression. Documentary street photography, 50mm lens, late-afternoon soft overcast light, realistic proportions and clothing. Background: softly out-of-focus workshop doorway and urban street, muted natural colors. Landscape composition with the person in the center, enough space around shoulders for responsive crop. No helmet, no sci-fi costume, no text, no logo, no watermark, no celebrity resemblance.
